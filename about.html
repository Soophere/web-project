About Nashaat

Our Story

Nashaat started as a small neighbourhood gym in Al Ansab. Like many small gyms in Oman, we used paper forms, a printed timetable and a WhatsApp group. Members often travelled to the gym for a class that was already full, and the timetable photo was hard to read.

This website fixes those problems. Prices, class times and trainers are now online, and members can see how many places are left in every class. We still keep reception and the phone working for anyone who prefers to register in person.

Our Mission

To make exercise easy, friendly and affordable for everyone in our community – beginners first.

Gym Rules

  1. Bring a towel and a water bottle.
  2. Wear clean sports shoes on the gym floor.
  3. Respect the class times:
    • Arrive 5 minutes before the class starts.
    • Cancel your booking if you cannot come, so someone else can use the place.
  4. Return weights to their place after use.
  5. Respect the women-only hours and women-only classes.

How to play
  1. Every exercise card hides its muscle group somewhere on the board.
  2. Press two cards. The same pair stays open and turns orange.
  3. Different cards close again after one second, and that costs you one move.
  4. Find all 6 pairs with as few moves as you can.

You get 3 free peeks: they show the whole board for two seconds. The card order is random, so every game is new.

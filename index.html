Welcome to Nashaat

Nashaat (نشاط) means activity in Arabic. We are a friendly local gym for men and women of all levels. Whether this is your first time in a gym or you have trained for years, our trainers will help you move, track your progress and improve.

Why train with us?

  • Clear prices – every plan is shown in Omani Rial, no hidden fees.
  • Online timetable – always up to date, no blurry photos in WhatsApp.
  • Book your place – every class has a fixed number of places.
  • Workout library – simple guides for beginners.
  • Men-only, women-only and mixed classes – clearly labelled.

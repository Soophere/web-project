Common Questions

Can I try the gym before I join?
Yes. Buy a Day Pass for 3 OMR at reception.
Who can get the Student plan?
Any university or college student with a valid student ID card.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yearly and Half Year members can freeze once for up to 30 days (for travel or illness).
Are there women-only times?
Yes. The gym floor is women-only every day from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and some classes are women-only.

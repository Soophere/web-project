Workout Library

Not sure what to do when you arrive at the gym? Start here. Each exercise shows the muscle group, the level, the steps and a safety note.

Please note: this library explains exercises only. It is general information and not medical advice. If you have a health condition or an injury, speak to a doctor before you start, and ask one of our trainers to check your technique.

Bodyweight Squat

Legs Beginner

How to do it:

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Push your hips back and bend your knees.
  3. Go down until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
  4. Push through your heels to stand up.

Safety: Keep your knees in line with your toes and your back straight.

Push-up

Chest Beginner

How to do it:

  1. Hands on the floor, a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Keep your body in a straight line.
  3. Lower your chest close to the floor.
  4. Push back up.

Safety: Start on your knees if a full push-up is too hard.

One-arm Dumbbell Row

Back Beginner

How to do it:

  1. Put one knee and one hand on a bench.
  2. Hold the dumbbell with the other hand.
  3. Pull the dumbbell up to your hip.
  4. Lower it slowly.

Safety: Do not twist your body. Use a light weight first.

Plank

Core Beginner

How to do it:

  1. Lie face down, then lift your body on your forearms and toes.
  2. Keep a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds.

Safety: Do not let your hips drop. Breathe normally.

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Shoulders Intermediate

How to do it:

  1. Sit on a bench with back support.
  2. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height.
  3. Press them up above your head.
  4. Lower them slowly.

Safety: Do not arch your lower back. Stop if you feel pain in the shoulder.

Biceps Curl

Arms Beginner

How to do it:

  1. Stand with a dumbbell in each hand, arms down.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl the weights up to your shoulders.
  4. Lower them slowly.

Safety: Do not swing your body to lift the weight.
